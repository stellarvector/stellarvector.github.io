***Stellar Vector*** is a primarily academic CTF team associated with KU Leuven, in the beautiful town of Leuven (coincidentally also the home of the Stella beer).
We welcome bachelor, master and PhD students, alumni, and any hacking aficionado located in Belgium, **regardless of your level of expertise or experience**.

We play CTFs roughly monthly and organise events to sharpen (y)our hacking skills.
