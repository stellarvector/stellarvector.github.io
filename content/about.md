## A decade and a half of CTFs

***Stellar Vector*** is the reboot of **HacknamStyle**, which was itself the reboot of a CTF team by the name of **The Electronic Mayhem**.
In short, we have been participating in CTFs for well over a decade.

The team is associated with KU Leuven and powered by the [DistriNet research group](https://distrinet.cs.kuleuven.be), but membership is not limited to researchers or even to students.

## Who can join

Everyone is welcome: bachelor, master and PhD students, alumni, and any hacking aficionado located in Belgium, regardless of level of expertise or experience.
You do not need to be good at this yet.

We play a CTF roughly every month, and organise events to further improve (y)our hacking skills.
